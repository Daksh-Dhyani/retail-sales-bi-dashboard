# Project Brief: Retail Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Problem Statement
A retail superstore wants to know which products, categories, regions and
customer segments drive sales and profit, and which ones underperform.

## Business Questions
1. What are total sales, profit, orders and average order value?
2. Which categories and sub-categories perform best and worst?
3. Which regions and states generate the most revenue and profit?
4. How do sales and profit change by month and year?
5. Where do discounts hurt profit?
6. What actions can the business take based on the evidence?

## Dataset
- Name: Superstore Dataset (Kaggle, user vivek468)
- Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final
- Size: 9,994 rows x 21 columns
- Raw file: data/superstore_raw.csv (never edited)

## Tools
Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn), SQLite, Power BI, Git/GitHub
